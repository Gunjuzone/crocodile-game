Practice question tags through fun guessing!

📋 How to Play
  • 1 Scan the QR code and open the app on your phone.
  • 2 Pick a category (Animals, Food, Jobs, or Hobbies).
  • 3 One student is the Dealer (sees the word). The other is the Guesser.
  • 4 The Guesser asks YES/NO questions using question tags to find the word!

Made for ESL classroom practice
